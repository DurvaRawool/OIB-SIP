Skincare Chatbot - DermaGPT

DermaGPT is an AI-powered skincare chatbot designed to provide personalized home remedy solutions. Users can interact with the chatbot to get:

  • Skin analysis based on a short questionnaire.
  • Personalized skincare routines for oily, dry, combination, or sensitive skin types.
  • Weather-specific, bridal, anti-aging, and daily skincare tips.
  • Remedies and guidance for common skin issues like acne, dryness, and pigmentation.
The chatbot is built using Django for backend management, with a modern and interactive frontend interface. Users can save their skin data and receive personalized advice whenever they interact with the chatbot.
